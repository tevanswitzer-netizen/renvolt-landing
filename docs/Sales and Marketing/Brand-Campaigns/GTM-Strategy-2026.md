# Renvolt — Go-To-Market Strategy 2026
### Brand Campaigns Reference
*Derived from: EV-Market-Research-2026.md · Updated: May 2026*

---

> **Purpose:** This is Renvolt's actionable 3-step GTM framework for the Alberta market. It defines our phased entry, the campaigns that support each phase, our core positioning angles, and the channels we invest in.

---

## Core Positioning Statement

> **"The charging network that actually works — especially in January."**

Renvolt does not compete on price. Renvolt does not compete on political alignment. Renvolt competes on **reliability, safety, and the premium experience** that Alberta EV drivers cannot find anywhere else on the highway.

---

## Brand Voice & Tone (Alberta Market)

| Principle | What It Means in Practice |
|---|---|
| **Practical, not preachy** | Never use "green", "eco", "sustainable" as primary messages |
| **Local, not corporate** | Speak like an Albertan, not a Bay Street press release |
| **Confident, not defensive** | Don't apologize for EVs; state the facts and let the numbers speak |
| **Peer-voiced** | User testimonials > brand claims, always |
| **Safety-aware** | Acknowledge the real concerns (cold, remote corridors); then solve them |

## Step 1 — OWN THE CORRIDOR (Months 0–12)

### Objective
Establish Renvolt as the undisputed premium DCFC brand on Alberta's highest-traffic inter-city routes before any competitor fills the gap.

### Priority Routes (Phase 1)
1. **Edmonton–Calgary** (highest volume; ~300km; 3 strategic plaza sites)
2. **Edmonton–Jasper** (most dangerous gap; highest anxiety route; 1–2 sites)
3. **Calgary–Banff** (high tourism volume; 1 site)

### Launch Campaign: "The Reliability Promise"
**Hook:** *"If Renvolt fails you on the highway, we cover your roadside. That's a promise no one else makes."*

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Press release to Edmonton Journal, CTV Edmonton, Global News Edmonton
- [ ] PlugShare listing for all Renvolt sites — optimized with photos, real-time status, cold-weather ratings
- [ ] ABRP (A Better Routeplanner) partnership — Renvolt sites appear in winter route planning as "reliability-verified"
- [ ] Outdoor billboard at highway on-ramps (QEW, QEII corridor) — simple, single message
- [ ] Google Maps / Apple Maps listings with photo sets

**Metrics for Step 1:**
- Site registrations on PlugShare within 30 days of launch
- Earned media mentions (target: 3+ local outlets)
- Uptime rate (target: 99%+ from day 1; publish monthly)
- First 500 charging sessions completed

## Step 2 — BUILD THE COMMUNITY MOAT (Months 3–18)

### Objective
Convert Renvolt's operational reliability into brand loyalty and peer-driven word-of-mouth. Make the Renvolt community the most trusted EV resource in Alberta.

### Campaign 2A: "Alberta EV Reality Check" Content Series

**Format:** YouTube + Newsletter + Social
**Cadence:** Bi-weekly video; weekly newsletter

**Content Pillars:**
1. **Winter Range Tests** — Real Renvolt users, real Alberta winter conditions, real range data
   - Example: *"We drove a Model Y from Edmonton to Jasper in January. Here's what actually happened."*
2. **Cost Comparisons** — Fuel savings calculations using Alberta electricity rates and local gas prices
   - Example: *"This Edmonton dad saved $2,400 last year. Here's his math."*
3. **Station Spotlights** — "What's at this Renvolt location?" — food, restrooms, WiFi, lighting
4. **Incentive Alerts** — Federal EVAP updates, utility rebates, employer programs

**Distribution:**
- YouTube (primary long-form)
- Instagram Reels / TikTok (cut-downs)
- Facebook (primary for 45–65 age group)
- Weekly email newsletter: **"Charged Weekly by Renvolt"**

---

### Campaign 2B: "Renvolt Pass" Launch

**Product:** Monthly subscription charging membership
- **Benefits:** Reserved stall guarantee (never arrive at a full plaza), rate-locked per-kWh pricing for 12 months, priority customer support
- **Pricing:** $19.99/month (break-even at ~4 sessions/month)
- **Target:** Power users and highway commuters; converts casual users to loyal members

**Launch Tactics:**
- [ ] Founding member offer — first 500 members get 3 months free
- [ ] AMA member exclusive offer — AMA partnership unlocks AMA's 1M+ Alberta member email list
- [ ] Email drip campaign to all PlugShare users who checked in at a Renvolt location

---

### Campaign 2C: AMA Partnership Initiative

**Goal:** Earn the "AMA Recommended" designation for Renvolt's charging network

The Alberta Motor Association is the single highest-trust automotive institution in the province. An AMA endorsement functions like a Consumer Reports seal for Albertan drivers.

**Approach:**
- Invite AMA to independently test Renvolt uptime data over a 90-day period
- Provide AMA members with an exclusive Renvolt Pass discount
- Co-produce an AMA-sponsored "EV Driving in Alberta Winter" guide featuring Renvolt infrastructure

### Recommended Marketing Channel Mix (Step 2)

| Channel | Priority | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **PlugShare / ABRP** | 🔴 Must-have | Zero cost; highest-intent EV audience; where drivers actually plan routes |
| **Local SEO** | 🔴 Must-have | "EV charger Edmonton to Calgary", "fast charger Red Deer" — intent-driven, compounding |
| **YouTube** | 🟠 High | Winter range content is uniquely shareable in Alberta; builds trust over time |
| **Facebook** | 🟠 High | Primary channel for 45–65 demographic; neighbourhood groups are high-influence |
| **Email Newsletter** | 🟠 High | Highest LTV channel once established; low cost; owned audience |
| **Google Search (Paid)** | 🟡 Medium | High CPC ($8–$25/click); use only for highest-commercial-intent keywords |
| **Radio (CHED, iHeart)** | 🟡 Medium | Reaches 50+ Alberta demographic during drive time; use for brand awareness |
| **OOH / Highway Billboard** | 🟡 Medium | High reach on QEII corridor; simple reliability message only |
| **Instagram / TikTok** | 🟢 Lower | Secondary to Facebook for core demo; primary for 28–42 urban millennial segment |

## Step 3 — CAPTURE B2B FLEET VOLUME (Months 9–24)

### Objective
Land institutional charging contracts that provide predictable volume, reduce dependence on organic consumer growth, and establish Renvolt as the enterprise-grade infrastructure partner for Alberta's fleet transition.

### Target Organizations (Priority Order)

| Organization | Type | Fleet Size Est. | EV Urgency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Government of Alberta fleet | Government | 3,000+ light-duty | ESG mandate; public optics |
| AHS (Alberta Health Services) | Healthcare | 1,500+ vehicles | ESG; employee demand |
| Suncor Energy | Oil & Gas | 2,000+ light-duty | Scope 3 emissions reporting |
| CNRL | Oil & Gas | 1,000+ light-duty | ESG investor pressure |
| University of Alberta | Education | 200+ fleet + campus fleet | Sustainability commitments |
| City of Edmonton municipal fleet | Government | 1,000+ | City electrification target |
| ETS (Edmonton Transit) | Transit | Bus electrification | Federal zero-emission bus funding |

### B2B Campaign: "Fleet Electrification. Made Operational."

**Core Offer:** Renvolt Fleet Depot Charging — design, install, and operate on-site charging infrastructure under a managed-services agreement.

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] **Fleet TCO Calculator** — Web tool showing 5-year savings for fleet electrification using Alberta ENMAX/ATCO rates; outputs a shareable PDF for CFO presentations
- [ ] **Fleet Electrification White Paper** — "How Alberta Organizations Are Electrifying Their Fleets Without Operational Disruption" — positions Renvolt as the expert
- [ ] **RFP Response Template Library** — Pre-built responses for government procurement processes
- [ ] **Reference Customer Strategy** — Win one institutional client in Year 1 (target: City of Edmonton or University of Alberta) to anchor all future B2B sales conversations

**B2B Outreach Channels:**
- LinkedIn (decision-maker targeting: Fleet Managers, VP Operations, Sustainability Directors)
- Direct email outreach to procurement contacts
- Chamber of Commerce Edmonton events
- EECC (Edmonton Electric City Committee) participation
- Alberta Municipalities conference sponsorship

### B2B Positioning Statement

> *"We make fleet electrification in Alberta actually operational — not just a line item on an ESG report. Renvolt designs, installs, and guarantees your depot charging infrastructure so your fleet runs and your CFO has one contract to sign."*
